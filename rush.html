WHY BETA?

More Than Four Years.

College is about more than classes. Beta gives you a community of people to experience it with — people with different majors, interests, backgrounds, and goals who become part of your everyday life.

RUSH

What to Expect

You don't need to know anything about fraternities before showing up. Rush is simply an opportunity to meet people and learn what Beta is about.

01

Show Up

Come to an event, meet some brothers, and get a feel for the chapter. No complicated preparation required.

02

Meet People

Talk to brothers with different majors, hobbies, backgrounds, and interests. Find the people you naturally connect with.

03

Ask Questions

Learn about brotherhood, academics, social life, costs, time commitments, leadership, and anything else you want to know.

04

Be Yourself

Rush isn't about trying to fit a mold. The whole point is figuring out whether Beta is somewhere you genuinely belong.
